Selection rationale
-------------------
The infrastructure model separates VMS servers from recording servers. VMS servers handle video management and GPU-based detector workloads, while recording servers handle camera recording only. The VMS servers are equipped with GPUs to support the detector workloads, and the recording servers are assigned cameras without GPUs. The cameras are distributed as evenly as possible across the servers to ensure balanced load.
