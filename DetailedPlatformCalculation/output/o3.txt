Selection and resource explanations
------------------------------------
Selected the listed recording-server processor with the highest upper CPU-load range; ties are resolved by the workbook's first-listed option. The same recommended platform supplies the VMS processor and baseline RAM; VMS RAM is increased to the required 32 GB minimum when the workbook baseline is lower.
Recording-server count is the minimum needed for the loaded specification's per-server limit; 118 cameras are distributed as evenly as possible across 2 recording servers.
The calculator recommends 1 detector GPU(s); the VMS tier has at least one server per recommended GPU. A VMS server without a detector GPU receives an NVIDIA GTX 1650.
Recording servers retain ownership of all camera groups and have no GPUs. VMS entries show received detector streams, not camera allocations.
